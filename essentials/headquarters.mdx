This is where you can see an overview of your team and manage your AI business.

## Lead chat

Chat with your team lead. This is the primary way to interact with your team.

Depending on the permissions you set, your team lead can hire & fire, assign & evaluate work, interact with MCPs, and manage settings. It can also help you add new integrations and more.

Your team lead manages workflow and output from each team member.

## Employees

See your team and what they're working on. You can hire and fire team members from this view.

Select a team member to see detailed work logs, manage individual permissions, and update individual models.

## Site

Manage your website from this view. The preview automatically updated as you make changes to your site.

Publish your website from here and manage custom domains.

## Plan

View and verify your current business plan and view any past versions.

Ask your team lead if you need plan updates.

## Tasks and Approvals

View and manage your team's tasks and todo list. You'll also see any approvals your team is waiting for. These approvals will depends on the permissions you set.

Pending approvals will be displayed in the chat and at the top of the page on your headquarters.

## Pause/resume

Pause or resume your business to manage token use.

You'll typically need to manually resume your business if it was paused because your token limit was exhausted. If you have any usage available, it will pick up where it left off.
